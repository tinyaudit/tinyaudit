On-device model auditing — what we measured

A model can pass a fairness check and still be unfair — you just have to look at its uncertainty.

TinyAudit audits small, compressed models for fairness, uncertainty, and explainability together — in the same memory budget the model runs in. Two findings, each read off a tracked, regenerable result file.

Finding 01 — Adult · logistic model

The fairness lenses disagree about who is worst served

Point-prediction fairness and calibration fairness rank the groups in a different order. Ranked by how often each racial group is selected (the usual fairness view), then by calibration error — how much the model's confidence can be trusted — the lines cross. White is the best-calibrated group yet one of the most-selected; Asian-Pac-Islander is the most-selected and the worst-calibrated. A demographic-parity-only audit sees none of this.

Finding 02 — Adult · neural net · pruning sweep

Compression can hide unfairness

As the network is pruned harder, it looks steadily fairer on demographic parity while its calibration collapses. Both are error-like quantities on the same 0–0.35 scale, so they share one axis. The parity gap falls to zero — not because the model became fair, but because it degraded into a near-constant predictor that treats everyone the same. A point-fairness audit would sign that off.

Read honestly — caveats

What each number does and doesn't mean

  • scalingFeatures are standardized first. Mixed-range columns (0/1 flags vs. tens-of-thousands) make the neural net unusable; scaling fixes it and also makes models sturdier under compression.
  • dataFolktables is a synthetic stand-in offline (its data needs a download); Adult and COMPAS are real. Trees have no weights to prune, so they sit out the compression grid.
  • int8Quantized models show blank uncertainty — their weights are locked in a format we can't peek inside to build the estimate, so we leave it blank rather than fake it.
  • compasHeavily-pruned COMPAS is degenerate — with only ~5 features it collapses to “predict the same for everyone,” which is trivially fair and meaningless.
  • methodThe uncertainty ensemble is 5 jittered copies of the one compressed model, not 5 from-scratch retrains — read magnitudes as relative. Exact reproducibility is Linux-only.
